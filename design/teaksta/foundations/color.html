Foundations

Colour

Four colours of the Sámi flag, each carrying exactly one meaning, over a warm paper neutral. Nothing here is decorative: if a word is blue it belongs to the topic, if it is green the learner got it right. No flag graphics anywhere.

Green

Correct. Nothing else is ever green.
--tk-green#007229Flag green. Score dots, correct-slot borders, solid fills.
--tk-green-strong#04581FGreen ink for a word answered right.7.3 : 1 on tint

Red

Wrong, and refused. Used sparingly and never alone.
--tk-red#D0402DFlag red. Marks and borders only — too light for small text.
--tk-red-strong#A32A1ARed ink: wrong answers, refusal messages.6.8 : 1 on paper
--tk-red-tint#FAE4DFThe band under a wrong pick, the refusal panel.

Yellow

Hint. The only warm accent, and it means help.
--tk-yellow#FFCE00Flag yellow. The hint button when it is being pressed.
--tk-yellow-strong#7A5400Hint ink. Yellow is never legible as text, so it darkens.6.0 : 1 on tint
--tk-yellow-tint#FFF0C4The revealed-lemma chip.

The mapping, at work

One colour, one meaning, everywhere in the app.
Blue#0035ADInteractive, and the topic. A word the grammar matched wears blue before the learner touches it.Mun oidnen viesu ikte.
Green#007229Correct. A pick that landed, a slot filled right, a dot on the score.Bárdni✓ lea skuvllas.

Every ink token above clears WCAG AA (4.5 : 1) against the surface it is specified for; --tk-line-control clears the 3 : 1 floor for control boundaries. --tk-red and --tk-yellow are deliberately not text tokens — they fill and outline, and their -strong siblings do the writing.
